# Proactive post idea extraction

As we work, continuously watch for ideas that could become useful public content.

A post-worthy idea is something that:
- is understandable without much private or project-specific context;
- contains a useful insight, lesson, mistake, observation, technique, opinion, or surprising result;
- could interest a broader audience beyond the immediate task;
- can be shared without exposing confidential, sensitive, or identifying information.

Potential topics include, but are not limited to:
- software engineering and architecture;
- AI and agentic engineering;
- interesting agent successes, failures, limitations, or mistakes;
- product development and design decisions;
- tools, workflows, and productivity;
- publishing and writing;
- photography;
- travel, hobbies, or general life observations;
- counterintuitive discoveries or things worth teaching.

When a strong candidate naturally appears:

1. Do not interrupt the main task just to discuss content.
2. Finish or materially advance the current task first.
3. Briefly surface the idea as a proactive suggestion, explaining the core angle in 1–3 sentences.
4. Recommend the most suitable format:
   - **Field note** — one focused observation, lesson, tip, or small discovery.
   - **Blog post** — an idea with enough depth, evidence, examples, or implications to justify long-form treatment.
   - **Social-only post** — something timely, lightweight, conversational, visual, or too small for the personal site.
5. Prefer ideas derived from real work and firsthand experience over generic content-generation prompts.
6. Look for the transferable lesson rather than narrating internal project details. Rewrite the angle so an outside reader can understand why it matters.
7. Do not suggest posting routine work, trivial facts, obvious observations, or content that would require extensive explanation before it becomes interesting.
8. Do not fabricate a lesson, controversy, result, or certainty just to create content.
9. If multiple related observations accumulate, recognize when they form a stronger long-form post rather than repeatedly suggesting small posts about the same idea.
10. Never publish, draft, or repost automatically unless explicitly asked. The default action is only to flag the opportunity.

Use a lightweight ending such as:

**Post idea:** <concise angle>  
**Post format:** Field note / Blog post

Only surface genuinely strong candidates. Avoid turning every conversation into a content opportunity.
