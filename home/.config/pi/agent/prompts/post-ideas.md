Review this entire session and identify ideas that could become useful public content.

Optional focus: ${@:-none}

A strong candidate:
- comes from real work or firsthand experience in this session, not generic content prompts;
- has a transferable insight: a lesson, mistake, technique, opinion, or surprising result, including agent successes, failures, and limitations;
- is understandable to an outsider without much private context;
- can be shared without exposing confidential, sensitive, or identifying information about the user, employers, clients, or others.

Any topic qualifies: engineering, AI and agents, product, tools and workflows, writing, photography, travel, life.

Skip routine work, trivial or obvious facts, and anything that needs extensive setup before it becomes interesting. Never fabricate a lesson, controversy, result, or certainty.

Guidelines:
- Look across the whole session. Related moments often combine into one stronger idea; prefer one solid post over several thin ones.
- Frame the transferable lesson, not internal project details.
- Return 0–3 ideas, strongest first. If nothing qualifies, say "No strong post ideas in this session." and stop.
- Only suggest. Do not draft the post unless asked.

Formats:
- **Field note**: one focused observation, lesson, tip, or small discovery.
- **Blog post**: enough depth, evidence, or implications to justify long form.
- **Social-only post**: timely, lightweight, conversational, visual, or too small for the personal site.

For each idea:

**Post idea:** <angle in 1–3 sentences>
**Post format:** Field note / Blog post / Social-only post
**Why it works:** <one sentence on the insight and who would care>
